What we learned

Current synthesis from the research packet. Exact records and complete source artifacts remain visible below.

Research conclusions

  • P06 established the adaptive learning mechanism, not the complete visual grammar.
  • Use controlled realistic screens, an outside option, per-dimension confidence and adaptive least-resolved-dimension targeting.
  • Learn continuous DesignDNA internally, but ship a complete pre-gated token pack; the exact axes, levels and 8–12 round estimate still require focused experiments.

What it owns

  • Preference axes and stimulus generation
  • A/B or multi-choice experiment policy
  • Outside option and re-roll behavior
  • Client design DNA and confidence
  • Preference update over time

Open first-principles questions

  • Is the mathematical minimum 7, 14 or another number of choices?
  • Which axes are independent?
  • Should choices use whole screens or controlled fragments?
  • How does preference confidence change by component context?

Inputs

  • Design stimuli
  • Client choices
  • Brand constraints

Explicit holds and blockers

  • COMMERCIAL DENOMINATOR THINLY VERIFIED — 13 of 103 observed. Consumer style quizzes are client-side JS behind bot protection (403 from Stitch Fix, Warby Parker, Looka, Behr, Hinge; JS shell from Function of Beauty; 404 from 1000minds, Conjointly). Needs a real browser session, not more fetching.
  • NO SUFFICIENCY GUARANTEE EXISTS for our setting. The 8-12 recommendation is a derivation plus five converging empirical sources, not a theorem. Gate 3 (simulate-to-size) must convert it into a measured number before it is quoted to the client as anything firmer.
  • DUAL-AUDIENCE PROBLEM UNSOLVED — for the 6 of 17 industries where `portal` is the secondary archetype, the person choosing (operator) is not the person using (end customer). No prior art found anywhere in the denominator. This is a design gap, not a research gap.
  • KNOB INDEPENDENCE ASSUMED, NOT MEASURED (Gate 1). The whole few-rounds argument rests on effective dimensionality being below 7.
  • 1000minds PAPRIKA UNVERIFIED (404 on both attempted paths). Its transitivity-based pair elimination is potentially directly applicable and should be verified before the format decision is frozen.
  • FORMAT DIVERGENCE from the lane owner's v1 ledger is live and unresolved by argument (P06-D01).
