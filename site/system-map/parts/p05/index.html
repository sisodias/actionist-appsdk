What we learned

Current synthesis from the research packet. Exact records and complete source artifacts remain visible below.

Research conclusions

  • The normalized local union is about 8,483 component identities; the source-bearing legacy store has 3,507 real components.
  • Replace inconsistent tags with canonical entities, aliases and measured visual clusters.
  • Own the archetype shell and core app primitives; harvest decorative surfaces. The layer is a snapshot, not yet living.

What it owns

  • Component ingestion and refresh
  • Dedupe and source linkage
  • Visual previews and interaction states
  • Taxonomy, tags and semantic use cases
  • Gallery search, comparison and selection

Open first-principles questions

  • What is the canonical component taxonomy?
  • How are near-duplicates clustered visually and structurally?
  • Which components are required for every archetype?
  • How are new 21st.dev releases discovered and re-indexed?
  • What metadata helps a client visually choose without understanding component names?

Inputs

  • 21st stores
  • New component feeds
  • Screenshots
  • Source metadata

Outputs

  • ComponentRecord
  • PreviewSet
  • CategoryGraph
  • Selection

Explicit holds and blockers

  • Rights clearance for both 21st stores. 21st.dev terms prohibit the acquisition mechanism; rights coverage is 2/11549 (0.017%); SISO harvest rule 6 makes both stores summarize-only. A counsel gate this lane records and does not discharge.
  • Whether a paid 21st.dev key returns component source. Determines whether the 5,004 bundle-only components can become source-bearing. Untested: requires a paid key and network calls, out of scope.
  • Visual near-duplicate rate. Floor measured at 102 surplus (1.3% of 7,678); name-collision ceiling 2,058 (25.9%). Range too wide to design against.
  • Corpus-wide theme-eligibility pass rate. Inherited rates are SAMPLED n=25/n=60.
  • Whether re-themed bundles look aesthetically right. Flagged by the prior audit as the most likely source of unpleasant surprises.
  • 42 commercial surfaces unreached due to a subagent usage limit; recorded as evidence_class unknown, not as absent.
