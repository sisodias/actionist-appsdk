03 / Synthesis

What this phase teaches us

The high-value result is a governed seam, not another builder feature list.

01 · SURFACE

The platform market is wider than the baseline

The original 67 surfaces now sit beside 50 first-party expansion candidates, but the important comparison fields are the gaps: authenticated behavior, authority, portability, rollback, support, and OEM continuity.

02 · ASSET

The laptop already contains a real corpus

The UI and repository material is not one magic “million repo” asset. It is a set of overlapping registries, indexes, archives, templates, and datasets with different rights and freshness states. The inventory tells us where to inspect next.

03 · DECISION

A repository is not a reusable block

The Block Contract makes the missing middle explicit: source and rights, typed ports, authority, state/replay, evidence, owner, maintenance, recovery, and an admission decision. Until those receipts exist, candidates stay reference-only or quarantined.

04 · VERTICAL

Start with a bounded read model

Across the 17-industry catalogue, operations, finance, CRM/lead, and support are useful archetypes. The safe pilot is synthetic and read-only; healthcare, legal, mortgage, HR, and other sensitive domains need stronger authority and legal evidence.

05 · MODEL

Cheap-model economics is still a hypothesis

The 20-task matrix now has thresholds and kill conditions, but no model, cost, latency, or safety result has been run. “Cheap” is not a claim until the same receipt captures version, tokens, repairs, side effects, and accepted outcome.

06 · POSTURE

Research is verified; product is not

The coordinator can now promote this research phase while keeping the parent goal active. The next work is source-room integration and an explicitly authorized synthetic pilot, not silent implementation or client-data access.
