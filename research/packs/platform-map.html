Headline

The build plane is converging. The actionable seam is authority, recovery, and evidence.

The lane compared Manus, Airtable Omni, Zapier Forms, Base44, Onlook, NocoBase, CopilotKit, v0, and Lovable. The evidence supports a constrained client build plane plus an agent-operation and evidence plane as a product direction. It does not establish a live Actionist implementation.

Do not collapse these states

  • Documented means a first-party URL and an observation date were recorded.
  • Authenticated/live is zero because no credentialed product session was available.
  • Implemented is zero because no Actionist runtime code was changed by this lane.
  • Candidate is not admitted; Horizon and screenshot-to-code remain held.

03 / The useful abstraction

Three planes, not one “AI builder” blob

The cross-lane comparison that survived review.

01 · build

Constrained client build plane

Known schema, known deployment boundary, registry-backed UI, bounded model edits, and a visible artifact trail.

  • Prompt → structured spec
  • Design direction → token contract
  • Scaffold → bounded adaptation
  • Build, smoke, visual proof
02 · operate

Authority / operation plane

The layer generic builders do not close: actions over long-tail or API-less software, with approvals, side-effect limits, retries, and recovery.

  • Typed authority boundary
  • Ask-human checkpoints
  • Idempotency and read-back
  • Rollback or compensating action
03 · evidence

Evidence / registry plane

Every source, transformation, dependency, license, test, visual baseline, owner, and release decision is inspectable.

  • Provenance and SPDX
  • Block manifest and SBOM
  • Decision ledger
  • Admission / quarantine state
Strategic conclusion: the three-plane wedge is supported as a product direction, unverified as a capability, and not claimed as implemented. That distinction is repeated throughout the original packets.

05 / The unresolved experiment

P-010 / screenshot-to-code admission

Candidate is held, not rejected.

Why it was selected

The lane saw a plausible path from screenshot-to-code into a registry-backed frontend assembly loop. The candidate remains valuable as a learning target because it tests the boundary between visual intent, token extraction, scaffold selection, and bounded adaptation.

Why it is not admitted

  • No isolated stack adaptation receipt.
  • No token-slot contract tied to a real registry.
  • No deterministic build or browser smoke exit.
  • No visual baseline or screenshot-diff receipt.
  • No owner, rollback, dependency, or asset-scan receipt.
Current verdict

Block Contract pilot: NOT AUTHORISED.

The research did not fail because the idea was bad. It stopped because the evidence ladder was incomplete. That is the point of the admission framework.
