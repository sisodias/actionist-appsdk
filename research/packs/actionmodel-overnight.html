02 / The honest readout

What the night changed

The evidence base is much richer. The completion claim is still deliberately withheld.

Solid gain

We now have a real research substrate.

The work is no longer a handful of agent summaries. It is a traceable local corpus with source links, hashes, counters, phase state, and repeatable smoke commands.

  • 17 industries × 10 dimensions are represented in the closure model.
  • 500 expansion candidates and 17,000 numeric matrix observations are preserved.
  • 67 company surfaces, 118 competitor surfaces, 144 feature keys, and 12 reusable atoms are catalogued.
Do not overclaim

Broad coverage is not complete depth.

The measured gap is the key finding: only 270 industry–repository pairs cover all ten dimensions. Most of the 17,000 slots are observations, not complete, rights-cleared, executable building blocks.

  • Rights, SBOM, support, OEM, runtime, rollback, and economics remain unknown or unexecuted.
  • The competitor taxonomy is 68 stated versus 144 enumerated, with no accepted denominator.
  • No client approval or block admission has occurred.
Design decision

The unit of value is a governed Block Contract.

The first-principles work converged on a niche → atom → block graph, with evidence ladders and explicit rights, owner, tenancy, cost, recovery, and admission fields.

  • Safe pilot shape: synthetic, read-only operations read-model.
  • Cheap-model use remains a falsifiable 20-task hypothesis, not a result.
  • Research artifacts are reference-only until the execution and admission gates run.

05 / Strategic signal

What the research actually taught us.

These are the decisions worth carrying into the next conversation.

Product

Build for bounded operating workflows first.

The public and competitor signals converge on CRM/lead work, inventory, internal tools, portals, finance operations, and support. The production cliff is integrations, auth/data/security, cost loops, and portability—not generating a pretty first screen.

Architecture

Retrieval and governance are part of the product.

A useful block needs provenance, rights, ownership, tenancy, cost, maintenance, recovery, and replay semantics. The Block Contract is the smallest governed unit that can carry those fields forward.

Research

Counts must separate breadth from depth.

17,000 observations, 500 candidates, 118 surfaces, and 144 feature keys sound large. The 270 complete-pair measurement is the more important truth because it says where the evidence is still shallow.

Next gate

Stop researching only when the evidence can survive execution.

  • Close or deliberately scope the 1,430 complete-pair gap.
  • Resolve taxonomy denominator and platform surface identity.
  • Run synthetic read-model receipts, rights/SBOM checks, evals, security, runtime, cost, and rollback gates.
  • Only then consider a governed block admission decision with client/legal inputs.
