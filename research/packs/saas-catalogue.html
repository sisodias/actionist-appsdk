Boundary that must not be lost

Existing systems can provide control references. The client ledger and Actionist evidence layer remain authoritative.

A catalogue entry is useful when it clarifies a data model, UI surface, workflow, or deployment substrate. It becomes risky when it silently becomes a dependency or an authority for money, consent, or client state.

Classification vocabulary

  • Whole system — potentially runnable substrate.
  • Reusable surface — bounded UI/data/workflow capability.
  • Reference — architecture or interaction pattern only.
  • Candidate — requires direct review and block gates.
  • Admitted — none yet.

03 / Vertical deep dives

Finance and CRM are not just rows in a catalogue

They received separate 440–540 line research lanes.

finance / 540 lines

Finance Operations

The finance lane built a 15-rank candidate matrix, local-first inventory, reusable-block mapping rules, and a non-negotiable control contract around evidence, reconciliation, approval, and the client ledger.

What it learned

  • Source-of-truth and reconciliation precede automation.
  • Evidence should be append-only and attributable.
  • Approval is part of the workflow, not a UI flourish.
  • OpenBooks/Accounted are control references only; no candidate is admitted.

04 / Reusable-block crosswalk

How candidates map to jobs without becoming authority

Candidate block → vertical atom → evidence gate.

inbox

Chatwoot / listmonk

Useful for communication, consent, SLA, and campaign events. The Actionist layer still owns approval, audit, and client-specific policy.

data

Teable / Baserow / Payload

Useful for read models and schema-bound CRUD. Write authority and migrations must be explicitly scoped.

workflow

Activepieces / n8n

Useful as workflow references. Connector actions and external effects need an Actionist authority contract.

calendar

Cal.diy / Hi.Events

Useful for schedule/attendee loops. Read-back and payment state remain direct-source questions.

CRM

Atomic / Frappe / ERPNext

Useful data-model references for lead, account, pipeline, and renewal. Avoid inheriting their whole product surface.

publish

Postiz

Useful approval→publish→report atom. The irreversible action requires explicit review and recovery.

Decision: use the catalogue to choose what to investigate and what can reduce build cost. Use the reusable-block gates to decide what may enter the library.
