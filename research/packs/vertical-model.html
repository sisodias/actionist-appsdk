02 / Reusable atom contract

The twelve atoms beneath the verticals

Defined in atoms-001.json; reused across crosswalk batches.

intake

Capture the job

intake_normalize turns a request into a stable shape; triage_route chooses owner, queue, or path.

understand

Make it structured

extract_structure pulls fields from documents/messages; classify_prioritize turns signal into order.

move

Coordinate the work

follow_up_chase and schedule_coordinate keep the process alive across people and systems.

control

Keep state trustworthy

reconcile_audit checks records; monitor_alert watches thresholds and exceptions.

communicate

Close the loop

report_digest makes progress legible; sync_handoff gives the next owner a durable state.

authority

Require the right human

approval_publish gates irreversible outcomes; browser_data_entry handles legacy/UI-only surfaces under explicit authority.

04 / Pilot archetypes

Three places to move from mapping to direct-source review

Scores are prioritisation signals, not customer validation.

28 / 35

Invoice-to-cash control

Finance-heavy document intake, extraction, follow-up, reconciliation, approval, and audit.

  • Strongest finance value.
  • Must define source of truth and allowed writes.
  • No production-write proof before direct review.
27 / 35

Request-to-dispatch desk

Operations intake, triage, schedule, chase, handoff, and exception reporting.

  • Broadest atom reuse.
  • Safest low-risk sandbox.
  • Good first synthetic-fixture candidate.
28 / 35

Lead-to-meeting pipeline

CRM/lead capture, qualification, follow-up, calendar coordination, consent, and read-back.

  • Strong value/frequency.
  • Outbound-send and opt-out controls mandatory.
  • CRM/calendar confirmation must be observed.
