destination.* The SISOCRM inspection tested that rule against a built estate and found it
**fails to discriminate** — Teable (absorbed natively) and Postiz (iframe-preserved) are both
capabilities. The estate's own recorded reason for the Teable native mount is
`THEMING-CONTRACT.md:94-102`: custom properties do not inherit across a cross-origin iframe.

S2-L3 therefore does **not** inherit the semantic rule. It builds the shell contract on the
mechanical axis the built estate actually used, and records the semantic rule as superseded.
