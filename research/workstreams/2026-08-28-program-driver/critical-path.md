# Critical path to one composed, measured product

The shortest credible path is a gated sequence. AFFiNE and Teable are parallel package probes, not a shortcut from research to production.

```text
S1/S2 converged research
        │
        ├── Framework Registry ─────────┐ VERIFIED_RESEARCH_PACKET
        ├── AFFiNE v0.1 package ───────┼ VERIFIED_PROTOTYPE_WITH_HOLDS
        └── Teable v0.1 package ───────┘ VERIFIED_PROTOTYPE_WITH_HOLDS
                         │
              convergence: all three prerequisites verified
                         │
              coordinator defaults fix workflow + Chatwoot candidate
              contract-only solver pilot gate OPEN
                         │
              three-candidate records + 22-rule solver run (authorized, not yet run)
                         │
              host/data/connector/runtime bindings + AssemblyPlan
                         │
              staged read-only binding (separate authorization)
                         │
              one measured end-to-end workflow
                         │
              qualification → human admission → release/learning
```

## Correct placement of AFFiNE and Teable

- AFFiNE is a reusable workspace/document seam probe. Its verified packet selects `intact_service` with a host adapter and tests host identity/session handoff, workspace selection, donor chrome, files/events and observability. It remains `NOT_QUALIFIED`/`NOT_ADMITTED` with H-THEME, H-SETTINGS, H-UPGRADE, H-DENSITY and H-CE-WEB open.
- Teable is a reusable structured-data seam probe. Its verified packet prefers an `intact_service` plus host-owned proxy/binding for v0.1 and preserves donor migration ownership, one-server/separate-schema compatibility, tenant isolation and incomplete authenticated-UI/attachment evidence. It remains `NOT_QUALIFIED`/`NOT_ADMITTED` with seven holds.
- They are not two production-admitted blocks or a complete product. Their verified packets now satisfy C1 and open E1; the third-shape candidate is Chatwoot and remains only a contract candidate until its own records and later gates pass.
- The third candidate must be materially different in reuse shape. The coordinator default is Chatwoot as an intact communication/service candidate. It remains unqualified and unadmitted until the pilot packet and later evidence gates pass.

## Stop conditions

Stop or redesign if the three records cannot expose compatibility without donor-specific leakage, if every candidate needs bespoke identity/data/navigation surgery, if the solver remains `UNDERDETERMINED` on critical fields, or if the measured assembly is slower or less maintainable than a focused custom build. None of those conditions may be silently averaged away.
