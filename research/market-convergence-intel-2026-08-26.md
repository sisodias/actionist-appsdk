## Crypto→SaaS conversion: no precedent

No clean case of a token community converting into SaaS subscription revenue exists.
Airdrop literature: successful airdrops retain 20-35% of claimants ACTIVE at 90 days —
retention of free on-chain activity, not $155/mo conversion. Freemium benchmarks: median
free→paid ~8%, AI-native 15-20% top end; usage-gated converts 1.5-2x feature-gated
(Actionist's one-agent desktop free tier is structurally right). Lindy killed its free
plan early 2026 over inference costs ("freemium death spiral").
