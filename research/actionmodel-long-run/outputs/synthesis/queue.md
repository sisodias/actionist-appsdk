## Review rubric

- `PASS` — direct evidence is present, current, reproducible, and scoped correctly.
- `PARTIAL` — useful evidence exists, but a source date, license, live capability,
  reproducibility, ownership, or rollback dimension is missing.
- `REJECT` — evidence contradicts the claim, the path/source is stale, or a hard
  boundary is crossed (for example, “candidate = admitted block”).
- `OPEN` — the evidence needed to decide is absent; name the exact gap.
