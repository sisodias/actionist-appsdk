## Access and confidence notes

- `x.com/ActionModelAI` returned a 403 in the research environment, so X counts/posts are from indexed snapshots and are not a complete archive.
- Instagram and TikTok were throttled or blocked; Linktree and official pages establish the handles, but not current counts.
- The Chrome Store exposes the aggregate rating/count. Extpose is useful because it mirrors a review subset, but it is not an authoritative export.
- Action Model’s own dashboards and milestone posts are valuable for understanding positioning and funnel design, but they should be reconciled against analytics exports before being used in a client recommendation.
