## Normalized receipt schema

- `repo_identity` preserves owner, name, URL, branch, and identity key from the Phase-3 candidate record.
- `public_metadata_source.recorded_urls` preserves the public URL identities already recorded in Phase 3; it is not a fetch receipt.
- `declared_license_signal` and `spdx_or_noassertion` preserve declared/API signals without clearing rights.
- `maintenance_signal` and dependency/SBOM fields are metadata/unknown signals only.
- `falsifier` and `next_clean_room_gate` are copied from the deterministic candidate record; they are future rejection/gate conditions, not executed results.
- `reference_only_boundary` makes non-execution, rights uncertainty, and non-admission machine-readable.
