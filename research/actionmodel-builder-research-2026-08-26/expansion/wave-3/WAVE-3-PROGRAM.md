## Purpose and immutable starting point

Wave 2 established a verified first floor: 170 exact industry×dimension cells,
10 observed repository slots per cell, and 1,700 combined observed slots. The
parent ledger still reserves 17,000 slots for 100 observations per cell. Wave 3
adds the next ten observation indices only; it does not rewrite wave 2, the
500-row tranche, or the 17,000-slot parent ledger.

```text
wave-2 combined floor: 170 cells × 10 = 1,700 observed
wave-3 delta floor:     170 cells × 10 = 1,700 new observations
wave-3 combined floor:  170 cells × 20 = 3,400 observed
parent target:          170 cells × 100 = 17,000 observed
```

The remaining 13,600 slots after this wave are an explicit research backlog,
not evidence that a cell lacks demand or capability.

## Required evidence contract

Every new observation or dossier claim must include:

- stable identity, exact industry/dimension or product-surface ID, and wave;
- observed date, direct URL/path, inspected source section, and evidence class
  `E`, `D`, `I`, or `U`;
- capability/atom relation, limitation, falsifier or next gate;
- source query/dossier provenance and rights/admission boundary;
- an explicit `observed`, `needs_direct_review`, `unobserved`, or `blocked`
  state where a receipt is missing.

Search snippets, vendor positioning, popularity, stars, marketplace presence,
and a declared license are not execution, safety, portability, or admission
proof. No output may claim a live Actionist contract, client value, license
clearance, deployment, rollback execution, or admitted block.
