## Evidence classes and readiness vocabulary

- Normative text: a cited standard, RFC, recommendation, or release defines requirements, terms, schemas, or version status.
- Implementation evidence: a local artifact, test receipt, signed provenance record, or reproducible result. A citation never substitutes for it.
- Observed: this packet records a source or design observation; it does not mean a target was executed.
- readiness_unexecuted: a bounded condition and falsifier are specified, but no execution or admission verdict exists.
- Access/rights gap: missing provenance, license, consent, authorization, retention, correction, or row-level access evidence remains unresolved.
