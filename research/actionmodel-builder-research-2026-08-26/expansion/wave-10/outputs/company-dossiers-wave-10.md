## Evidence and normalized field contract

Each detailed row carries surface ID, current status, HTTP/source reachability, observed date, evidence class, current feature/lifecycle receipt, changed or unchanged fields, limitation, priority reason, and falsifier. The normalized matrix uses:

`P` prompt/context; `A` artifact; `D` data/auth; `X` integrations; `V` UI assembly; `O` source ownership; `IO` import/export; `R` deployment/runtime; `$` usage/economics; `Ap` approval/authority; `Au` audit/evidence; `Rb` rollback/recovery; `M` maintenance/retention; `S` support/SLA/security; `OEM` OEM/white-label; `C` continuity; `Ex` exit.

Evidence classes:

- `E` — the cited first-party URL or page was directly inspected on 2026-08-26.
- `D` — a first-party source documents the claim; this is not authenticated behavior.
- `I` — comparative inference from the current source and immutable public/standards packets.
- `U` — missing, private, gated, contract-only, redirected without migration proof, thin, or not behaviorally authenticated.

HTTP `200` means the bounded URL probe received a successful response. It does not mean the page contained all requested fields. HTTP `403`/`429` and reader errors remain explicit access states.
