## Evidence classes and readiness vocabulary

- Normative text: the cited standard or RFC defines requirements, terms, or schemas. It does not establish that this repository implements them.
- Implementation evidence: a repository artifact, test receipt, signed provenance record, or reproducible local result. None is inferred from a standard citation.
- Observed: this packet contains a source or design observation. It does not mean a target scan, eval, contract, deploy, or admission was run.
- readiness_unexecuted: a bounded readiness condition and falsifier are specified, but no execution or admission verdict exists.
- Access/rights gap: missing provenance, license, consent, authorization, retention, correction, or row-level access evidence remains a blocker to any later admission decision.
