## Purpose and immutable starting point

The parent ledger reserves 17,000 slots: 17 industries × 10 dimensions × 100
observations. Wave 5 adds only observation indices 31–40 to the verified Wave 4
floor.

```text

wave-5 delta:       170 cells × 10 = 1,700 new observations

parent target:      170 cells × 100 = 17,000 observed
```

The remaining slots are a research backlog, not evidence that a cell lacks
demand, capability, or a viable product opportunity. Parent, tranche, Waves
2–4, and all prior lane artifacts are immutable inputs to this wave.

## Evidence contract

Every row, dossier field, and synthesis assertion must carry a stable identity,
exact industry/dimension or surface ID, wave/index, observed date, direct
URL/path, inspected section, evidence class (`E`, `D`, `I`, or `U`), atom or
capability relation, limitation, falsifier/next gate, provenance, and
rights/admission boundary. Search snippets, stars, marketplace presence,
vendor positioning, declared licenses, and generated scaffolds are not proof of
execution, safety, portability, authority, or adoption. Missing, gated,
contradictory, sponsored, or no-result evidence remains an explicit gap.
