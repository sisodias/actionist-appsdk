The central conclusion is now clearer: Action Model should not compete on
prompt-to-screen generation alone. The durable opportunity is a governed
assembly and operations layer that can select source-cleared primitives, bind
them to a client-owned data/authority boundary, prove the result, and recover
when the generated application or external system behaves unexpectedly.

## What each lane actually achieved

The packet adds 36 product surfaces and takes the combined census from 31 to
67 rows. It covers full-stack builders, enterprise low-code, design-to-code,
coding agents, browser/RPA, workflow/execution, data-app workspaces, and
sandbox/runtime surfaces. It records first-party pricing or usage units,

## Cross-lane findings

1. **Generation is the crowded layer.** Prompt → context → generation → preview
   → publish is widely represented and is not, by itself, a defensible moat.
2. **The last mile is the buying criterion.** Auth, data ownership,
   integrations, permissions, tenancy, security, mobile handoff, portability,
   cost ceilings, observability, and rollback recur across vendor and user
   evidence.
3. **The reusable unit is an atom plus contract, not a repository.** A block
   must declare its host/data/UI/action/evidence ports and its authority,
   verification, recovery, audit, rights, and owner boundaries.
4. **Vertical specialization is a contract problem.** The same `follow_up` or
   `reconcile` atom changes materially across accounting, healthcare,
   construction, education, ecommerce, CRM, support, and logistics because the
   source of truth, authority, sensitivity, and recovery rules change.
5. **Cheap-model economics remain a falsifiable hypothesis.** Credits, tasks,
   compute seconds, browser hours, ACUs, LOC, and per-conversation pricing are
   not comparable until measured against accepted outcomes, repair rounds,
   evidence completeness, and escalation.
6. **Portability needs receipts.** “Code download” or “export” does not prove
   backend parity, migrations, secrets, auth, jobs, assets, licenses, data
   ownership, or reproducible deployment.
7. **The first engineering decision is governance.** The Actionist canonical
   contract, source-rights/SBOM execution, synthetic model eval, security
   probes, and client economics/legal inputs are still blockers to admission.
