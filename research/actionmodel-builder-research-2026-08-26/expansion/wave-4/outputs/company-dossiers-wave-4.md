## Evidence and field contract

The normalized fields are abbreviated in the matrix to keep 67 rows reviewable:

`P` prompt/context; `A` generated artifact; `D` data/auth; `X` integrations; `V` UI assembly; `O` source ownership; `IO` import/export; `R` deployment/runtime; `$` usage/economics; `Ap` approval/authority; `Au` audit/evidence; `Rb` rollback/recovery; `OEM` OEM/white-label; `C` continuity; `Ex` exit.

Evidence classes:

- `E` — the cited URL was directly inspected during this Wave 4 pass on 2026-08-26. `E/D` means the accessible page also documents the vendor claim.
- `D` — documented first-party claim retained from the current page or immutable prior packet; not authenticated behavior.
- `I` — comparison or inference from the cited evidence and the prior portability/standards packets.
- `U` — not found, gated, private, contract-only, region/account limited, redirected without migration proof, or not authenticated.

No row says that Actionist implements, admits, licenses, deploys, or safely executes any vendor capability.
