## Purpose and immutable starting point

Wave 3 is coordinator-verified at 3,400 observed repository slots: 170 exact
industry×dimension cells with 20 observations each. The parent ledger reserves
17,000 slots for 100 observations per cell. Wave 4 adds only observation
indices 21–30, taking the joined floor to 30 per cell. Parent, tranche-1,
wave-2, and wave-3 files are immutable inputs.

```text

parent target:          170 cells × 100 = 17,000 observed
remaining after wave 4:  11,900 reserved slots
```

The remaining slots are a research backlog, not evidence that a cell lacks
demand, capability, or a viable product opportunity.

## Required evidence contract

Every new row, dossier claim, or synthesis assertion must include:

- stable identity, exact industry/dimension or product-surface ID, and wave;
- observed date, direct URL/path, inspected source section, and evidence class
  `E`, `D`, `I`, or `U`;
- capability/atom relation, limitation, falsifier or next gate;
- query/dossier provenance and rights/admission boundary;
- explicit `observed`, `needs_direct_review`, `unobserved`, or `blocked` state
  when a receipt is missing.

Search snippets, stars, marketplace presence, vendor positioning, declared
licenses, and generated scaffolds are not execution, safety, portability,
authority, or admission proof. No output may claim a live Actionist contract,
client value, license clearance, deployment, rollback execution, or admitted
block.
