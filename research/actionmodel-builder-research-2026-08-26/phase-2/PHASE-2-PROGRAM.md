## Purpose

The numeric matrix is complete, but the original brief is not. This phase
turns the matrix and existing packets into deeper platform dossiers, a real
inventory of local corpora, industry-specific specifications, a canonical
Block Contract, and executable-gate designs. Workers must preserve evidence,
source quality, rights, falsifiers, and unknowns instead of compressing them
into unsupported conclusions.

## Boundaries

- Read-only local discovery unless a worker writes its own phase-2 artifact.
- No deletion, cleanup, moving, cloning, building, deployment, client-data access, vendor login, or external side effect.
- Do not copy repository code or treat stars, demos, pricing, licenses, or public case studies as admission proof.
- Keep first-party, empirical, community, vendor, inferred, gated, stale, and unknown evidence classes separate.
- Preserve exact URLs, local paths, ownership/rights state, freshness, hashes, and falsifiers.
- Each worker updates only its own phase-2 lane object. The coordinator owns shared phase-2 promotion after independent audit.

## Lane contracts

### RCH-PLATFORM-DEEPDIVES

Deep-dive the current 67 company/product surfaces and expand the platform
universe using a repeatable search protocol. For every surface, record the
actual capability boundary across prompt/design/repository input, data and
auth, agent/tool authority, API/MCP, import/export, deployment, tenancy,
pricing/usage, audit, portability, maintenance, support, rollback, lifecycle,
and OEM/white-label. Separate first-party evidence from inference and mark
authenticated behavior as unknown when it was not tested.

Outputs: `outputs/platform-deepdives.md` and a machine-readable register if
needed. No account-level or production claim is allowed.

### RCH-LOCAL-CORPUS

Perform a read-only local-laptop inventory for material associated corpora:
21st.dev/UI hubs, component registries, larger GitHub/open-source indexes,
AutoSAS material, private-builder archives, research packs, and reusable
templates. Inspect names, manifests, sizes, timestamps, ownership/license
signals, duplicate relationships, and likely relevance without opening or
executing untrusted code. Record missing/permission-limited paths explicitly.

Outputs: `outputs/local-corpus-inventory.md` and
`outputs/local-corpus-register.jsonl`. Do not delete, move, or alter discovered
files.

### RCH-INDUSTRY-SPECS

Convert the 17 industry profiles, 12 teams, 66 use cases, 72 ideas, 12 atoms,
and 100-per-cell evidence floor into bounded industry specifications. For each
industry identify the highest-value read-only workflow, entities, states,
terminal owner, authority boundary, source-of-truth system, exception paths,
evidence needed, and why it is or is not a safe pilot. Preserve the distinction
between demand signal and validated demand.

Output: `outputs/industry-atom-specifications.md`.

### RCH-BLOCK-CONTRACT

Draft the canonical Actionist Block Contract v1 from the first-principles and
standards packets. Define block identity, atom, input/output/data/UI/action
ports, state machine, idempotency/replay, authority and consent, provenance and
rights, evidence receipts, owner, tenancy, cost, maintenance, recovery, and
admission status. Include a small JSON schema/fixture if useful, but do not
implement runtime behavior or admit any block.

Outputs: `outputs/block-contract-v1.md` and
`outputs/block-contract-v1.json` if the schema is machine-readable.

### RCH-EVAL-ADMISSION

Turn the seven admission gates into a falsifiable execution plan. Define
synthetic read-only fixtures for operations, finance, CRM/lead, and support;
the 20-task cheap-model comparison; model/version/cost recording; negative
paths; security and authority probes; license/SBOM checks; visual and runtime
proof; portability; rollback; maintenance; and client/legal approval inputs.
Specify pass/kill criteria and receipt schemas. Designs are not executed until
the canonical contract and explicit implementation authorization exist.

Output: `outputs/evaluation-and-admission-plan.md`.

## Acceptance gates

1. Each lane writes its contracted artifact(s) with sources, counts, unknowns, and falsifiers.
2. Platform and local-corpus claims are path/source-backed and do not imply exhaustive coverage without a stated search boundary.
3. Industry specifications are bounded and distinguish demand from execution proof.
4. The Block Contract is versioned, internally coherent, and remains `not_admitted` for all candidate blocks.
5. Evaluation/admission plans contain fixtures, negative cases, thresholds, kill criteria, evidence IDs, and explicit unexecuted markers.
6. All lane callbacks and post-write smokes are verified before shared phase state is promoted.
