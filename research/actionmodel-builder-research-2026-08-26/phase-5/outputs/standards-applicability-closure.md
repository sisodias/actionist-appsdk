# Standards-to-Block-Contract applicability closure — Phase 5

## Closure status

This is a research-only applicability closure. It maps documented specifications, standards, protocols, and evidence requirements to the versioned Block Contract v1 fields; it does not establish conformance, certification, executed scans, runtime receipts, security results, legal clearance, production readiness, implementation, or admission.

- Cells: 170 exact unique industry × dimension records (17 industries × 10 dimensions).
- Readiness: 170 observed packet mappings; 170 `readiness_unexecuted`; 0 admitted.
- Sources: 70 unique W11 direct source identities retained in the source register; records cite relevant source IDs and URLs.
- Source access: inherited W11 aggregate is 23×200, 45×206, and 2×403; no per-URL status is newly asserted and no source was fetched in this lane.
- Boundary: execution status `UNEXECUTED`; admission status `NOT_ADMITTED`; implementation false; client/private data false; authenticated capability and production deployment claims false.
