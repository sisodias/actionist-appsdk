- `joins` preserve industry IDs, matrix cells/slots, evidence class, observation count, and target-cell metadata as recorded by Phase 3. Joins are prioritization context, not capability proof or validated demand.
- `falsifier` and `next_clean_room_gate` are bounded future checks. They do not authorize the checks or imply that they ran.
