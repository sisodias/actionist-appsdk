## Ranking method

Ranking is reproducible from Phase-2 fields. Existing records start at 60 points; candidates start at 42. Additions: +14 for E/U evidence, +10 for access/lifecycle discontinuity, up to +12 for unresolved requested fields, +6 for rights/OEM/support/exit gaps, +5 for agent/runtime authority relevance, +8 for candidate source gates, +8 for high Action Model adjacency, and +4 for design/builder adjacency. Related-existing candidates receive −10. Bands: P0 ≥88, P1 ≥72, P2 ≥55, P3 below 55. Ties sort by source record type and source record ID.

Priority means information value and risk reduction for the next source read; it does not mean product quality, market success, safety, portability, or admission readiness.
